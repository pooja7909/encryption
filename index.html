Caesar cipher wheel

The blue outer ring is plaintext, and the red inner disk is ciphertext. Change the shift and the red disk turns. Then find a letter on one ring and read its partner on the other.

Shift key

+3

Tip: drag the wheel to turn the red disk by hand. Tap any letter in the result or the strip to light it up on the wheel. A negative shift turns the disk the other way. For example, −3 gives the same wheel as +23.
